# Capacity per edge and tier

Generated by `benchmarks/report_capacity.py`. *Max sustained* is the highest offered rate a majority of replicates sustained (≥95% placed, ≤1% dropped, ≤0.5% failed, worst GET p95 under the SLO), as served requests/s. *Binding* is the component using the largest share of its pinned cores at the first failing rate, with that share; *below 85%* means it was busiest without being pinned at its limit — a single-worker origin queues well before 100% — and the host-wide busy share (kernel networking is charged to no container) is the next suspect. *Edge-CPU-bound* is edge cores ÷ edge CPU per request at the highest sustained rate — the throughput the edge's own cores would allow, whatever else saturates first.
